# Nigerian Agricultural Slang, Brand Names & Colloquialisms Reference

This reference helps the agent interpret informal, vernacular, and messy WhatsApp messages from Nigerian farmers and agro-dealers.

---

## 1. Common Herbicides & Nicknames

| Farmer Slang / Common Brand | Actual Active Ingredient | Type | Typical Use & Agronomic Rules |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **"Force Up", "Roundup", "Clearweed", "Sunphosate", "Sarosate"** | Glyphosate 480 SL | Non-selective Systemic | Land preparation only. **NEVER spray on standing crops** (will kill maize, tomato, pepper, cassava foliage). |
| **"Gramoxone", "Dragon", "Relief", "Paraquat"** (Note: Paraquat is restricted/phased out by NAFDAC) | Glufosinate-ammonium 200 g/L or Diquat / Contact alternative | Non-selective Contact | Rapid weed burndown, inter-row directed spray with hood. |
| **"Atrazine", "Maize Herbicide", "Atrasun"** | Atrazine 500 SC / 80 WP | Selective Pre & Early Post | Safe on Maize and Sorghum only. **Kills legumes and vegetables**. |
| **"Nicomaize", "Sentrane", "Nicosulfuron"** | Nicosulfuron 40 SC | Selective Post-emergence | Controls annual grasses in standing Maize (2–6 leaf stage). |
| **"2,4-D", "Aminoforce", "U-46"** | 2,4-D Amine Salt 720 SL | Selective Post-emergence | Kills broadleaf weeds in Maize, Rice, Sugarcane. High drift hazard for nearby vegetables/tomatoes! |
| **"Lagon", "Dual Gold", "Metolachlor"** | S-Metolachlor + Terbuthylazine / Isoxaflutole | Pre-emergence | Applied within 48 hrs of planting Maize/Cassava. Requires moist soil. |
| **"Select", "Fusilade", "Targa Super"** | Fluazifop-p-butyl / Quizalofop | Selective Post-emergence | Controls grasses in broadleaf crops (Cassava, Cowpea, Tomato, Groundnut). |

## 2. Common Insecticides & Terminology

| Farmer Slang / Common Brand | Active Ingredient | Target Pest & Caution |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **"Sniper", "DDVP", "Dichlorvos"** | Dichlorvos 1000 EC | **BANNED for food crops/storage**. If customer asks for Sniper for farm use, recommend safe crop alternatives (e.g., Emamectin benzoate, Cypermethrin). |
| **"Ampligo"** | Chlorantraniliprole + Lambda-cyhalothrin | Armyworm in maize, Tuta absoluta in tomato. High-value systemic. |
| **"Best Action", "Rocket", "Attack"** | Cypermethrin + Dimethoate | Broad spectrum chewing & sucking pests. Strong odor, standard PPE required. |
| **"Belt", "Proclaim", "Emamectin"** | Emamectin Benzoate 5% WDG / Flubendiamide | Fall armyworm, caterpillar, stem borer. Low volume requirement. |
| **"K-Othrine", "Deltamethrin"** | Deltamethrin | Storage / grain protection or vegetable pests. |

## 3. Fungicides & Soil Inputs

| Farmer Slang / Common Brand | Active Ingredient | Target Disease |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **"Red powder", "Seed dressing"** | Thiram + Permethrin / Metalaxyl | Seed treatment before planting. |
| **"Mancozeb", "Dithane", "Yellow powder"** | Mancozeb 80% WP | Preventative contact fungicide for Early/Late Blight in tomato & potato. |
| **"Ridomil", "Banko", "Metalaxyl-Mancozeb"** | Metalaxyl + Mancozeb | Systemic + Contact for Downy mildew and severe Blight. |
| **"Force / Furadan substitute"** | Carbofuran replacement (e.g. Cartap, Fipronil granules) | Soil pests, termites, nematodes. |

## 5. Units, Packaging & Measurements

| Nigerian Informal Term | True Metric Equivalent | Interpretation Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **"Cartoon" / "Carton"** | 12 × 1L bottles or 20 × 1L or 24 × 500ml | Check specific product carton pack size. |
| **"Sachet" / "Small pack"** | 50g, 100g, or 10g/25g sachet | Usually seeds or wettable powder fungicides. |
| **"Tin"** | 50g or 100g canned seed container | Common for hybrid vegetable seeds. |
| **"Knapsack"** | 16-litre or 20-litre manual/battery sprayer | Used to measure dosing: e.g., "1 milk tin per knapsack" (~150ml). |
| **"Acre" / "Hectare"** | 1 Hectare = 2.47 Acres = 10,000 m² | 1 Hectare usually requires 4 to 5 litres of Glyphosate or Atrazine. |
