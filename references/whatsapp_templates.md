# Farmsquare WhatsApp Conversational Templates

These battle-tested WhatsApp messaging templates are tuned for high customer response rates, clear communication, fraud prevention, and rapid payment conversions in Nigeria.

---

## 1. Disambiguation & Clarification Template
*Use when the customer sends a fragmented, misspelled, or vague request.*

> **Hello [Customer Name / Chief / Boss],**
> 
> Thank you for reaching out to **Farmsquare**! 🌱
> 
> To make sure I get you the exact right chemical and quantity for your farm:
> 1. Which crop are you spraying or planting? (e.g., Maize, Cassava, Tomato)
> 2. Are the crops already growing on the field, or is this for clearing before planting?
> 3. How many hectares/acres or how many cartons/litres do you need?
> 4. Where is your farm or delivery location located (Town & State)?
> 
> Once you let me know, I will calculate the exact quantity and give you the best price right away! 🚜

## 2. Agronomic Safety Warning Template
*Use when a customer orders a dangerous or damaging combination (e.g. Glyphosate on standing crops).*

> ⚠️ **Important Agronomic Notice for your farm, [Customer Name]:**
> 
> You mentioned you want **[Product Name, e.g. Force Up / Glyphosate]** for weed control on your **[Crop, e.g. Maize / Tomato]** field.
> 
> Please note that **[Product Name]** is a *non-selective systemic herbicide*—it kills green crops completely upon contact! If you spray it over your growing crops, it will damage them.
> 
> ✅ **Recommended Safe Solution:**
> For standing [Crop], we recommend **[Safe Alternative, e.g., Atrazine 500 SC / Nicosulfuron]**. This kills the stubborn weeds without harming your crop at all.
> 
> Would you like me to switch your quote to the safe alternative?
